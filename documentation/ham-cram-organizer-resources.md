## Suggested use sequence for a no-experience organizer

1. Read Part 97 and pick a VEC from the NCVEC list.
2. Complete VE accreditation steps with your chosen VEC (for example ARRL, W5YI, or GLAARG).
3. Build your session workflow in ExamTools (or your VEC-required platform), then run a dry run with your VE team.
4. Use current NCVEC question pool releases and errata to align class content and practice materials.
5. Publish the exam session and direct students to FRN registration and study resources before class day.
